# PromptBR — Brand & Product Direction 2026

## Positioning

**PromptBR — IA útil, aplicada ao trabalho real.**

Not a news race, prompt dump or model leaderboard. PromptBR is a practical layer between AI capability and a real task.

## Personality

- curious, not breathless;
- practical, not simplistic;
- technically aware, not exclusionary;
- Brazilian, not translated corporate copy;
- experimental, but explicit about uncertainty.

## Visual system

### Core colors

- Ink: `#07100E`
- Deep surface: `#0F1C18`
- Acid signal: `#D6FF4B`
- Mint: `#7CE1BC`
- Sky: `#7CC9FF`
- Paper: `#F2EFDF`

The acid color means **action / signal / useful next step**, not decoration.

### Typography

- Space Grotesk — headings and strong navigation;
- Manrope — reading and interface;
- DM Mono — labels, taxonomy and system states.

### Mark

The new mark combines a command/prompt chevron, cursor line and a small signal dot inside a rounded module.

## Product pillars

1. **Prompts** — immediate execution.
2. **Tools** — choosing the right capability.
3. **Guides** — understanding before adopting.
4. **Newsletter** — owned recurring distribution.
5. **Labs** — first-party experiments/products only when they are public and testable.

## Editorial test

Before publishing, ask:

1. What concrete task does this help with?
2. What is measured vs inferred?
3. What can go wrong?
4. Is the information time-sensitive?
5. What should the reader do next?

If there is no useful next action, the topic probably does not deserve a page yet.
